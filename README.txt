1. FOLDER STRUCTURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

- menu.html     <-- Full Menu & Shopping Cart
- reservation.html <-- Table Booking Form

- style.css     <-- Main Design & Styling
- script.js    <-- Logic for Cart & Animations
- logo.png      <-- Replace this with your own logo

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO CUSTOMIZE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. CHANGING THE LOGO:
Simply replace the 'logo.png' in the root folder with your own logo. 
Make sure your logo is also named 'logo.png' or update the <img> tags 
in the HTML files.

B. CHANGING IMAGES:
Move your own photos into the 'assets/images' folder. 
To make it easy, rename your photos to match these names:
- hero.jpg (Main Header)
- pasta.jpg (Menu Preview)
- appetizer.jpg (Menu Preview)
- truffle_fries.jpg (Menu Item)
- calamari.jpg (Menu Item)
- steak.jpg (Menu Item)
- lobster.jpg (Menu Item)
- arrabiata.jpg (Menu Item)

C. CHANGING PRICES & TEXT:
Open any .html file in a text editor (like Notepad++ or VS Code). 
Search for the current price (e.g., ₦8,200) and type in your own.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MAKING THE FORMS WORK (Email setup)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This template is integrated with Formspree (free).
1. Go to https://formspree.io and create a free account.
2. Create a "New Form" and copy your unique "Form ID".
3. Open 'index.html', 'reservation.html', and 'contact.html'.
4. Search for 'YOUR_FORMSPREE_ID' and replace it with your actual ID.
Example: action="https://formspree.io/f/xbjvnkpk"
